About Me

Hi, there! I’m Dengyang Jiang, currently a Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, mainly supervised by Prof. Harry Yang.

My research interests encompass deep learning and computer vision.

I’m delighted to communicate and collaborate with anyone interested in this field. Feel free to reach out via email or RedNote.
